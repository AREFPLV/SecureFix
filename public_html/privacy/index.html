What the forms collect

The repair booking, call-out, trade-in, recycling quote, lifecycle quote and Protect consultation forms collect your name, email, phone, device and fault. The call-out form also collects your address and postcode. You may add notes about the job.

Why we use it

We use those details to reply to you and to carry out the booking, quote, trade-in or recycling request you sent. We do not sell personal data.

How the form is sent

Repair booking, call-out, trade-in, recycling, business IT quote and Protect consultation forms are sent through FormSubmit to repair@securefix.uk. FormSubmit receives the fields you submit so the message can be delivered to SecureFix.
